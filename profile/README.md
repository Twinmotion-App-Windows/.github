## 🚀 At a Glance
Twinmotion for Windows is a standalone real-time visualization tool that shortens the distance between a 3D model and a compelling presentation. Import your geometry, dress it with materials and vegetation, and explore the space live before exporting the results. It is built for architects, planners, and interior and landscape designers who want fast, believable visuals without managing a complex render pipeline.

## ✨ What You Get
| Feature | What it does for you |
|---------|----------------------|
| Real-time engine | Renders lighting, shadows, and reflections live as you edit the scene |
| Model import and direct link | Brings in geometry from CAD and BIM tools like Revit and keeps it in sync |
| Asset library | Adds plants, people, vehicles, and materials with drag-and-drop |
| Environment controls | Adjusts sun, weather, seasons, and time of day in seconds |
| Media export | Outputs high-resolution images, panoramas, and walkthrough videos |

## 🎯 Quick Start
1. Import a model, or link directly to your Twinmotion Revit workflow and other design tools.
2. Drag materials, plants, and objects onto the scene.
3. Set the lighting, weather, and season to match your vision.
4. Move through the model in real time to frame your shots.
5. Export images, panoramas, or a walkthrough video to share.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Twinmotion free?**
A free version and a free trial have been offered, so you can start without paying, while advanced or commercial use may need a paid license. Verify the current terms on the official Twinmotion website before your download.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Twinmotion runs on modern 64-bit Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11, and benefits greatly from a dedicated graphics card.

**Is my data secure?**
Your projects are stored locally on your Windows computer, so you keep control of your files. Back up regularly and secure your account to protect your work.
